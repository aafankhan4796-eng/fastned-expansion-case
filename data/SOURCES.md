# Data sources

| Field | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `bev_registrations_2025`, `bev_growth_vs_2024_pct` | ACEA, [New car registrations, December 2025](https://www.acea.auto/files/Press_release_car_registrations_December_2025.pdf) | Full-year 2025 battery-electric car registrations |
| `bev_share_2025_pct` | Calculated: BEV registrations / total new car registrations (same ACEA release) | |
| `dc_charge_points_2023` | ACEA, [Charging ahead (Apr 2024)](https://www.acea.auto/files/Charging_ahead_Accelerating_the_roll-out_of_EU_electric_vehicle_charging_infrastructure.pdf), p. 8 (EAFO data) | All public DC points, any power level, end-2023 |
| `bev_fleet_share_2024_pct` | ACEA, [Vehicles on European roads 2026](https://www.acea.auto/publication/report-vehicles-on-european-roads-2026/), p. 16 | Share of total car fleet. Italy/Sweden rows should be double-checked |
| `bev_fleet_est_2024` | Estimate: fleet share x total car fleet (same report), rounded | Estimate, not a reported figure |
| `policy_score` (1-3) | Author's judgement based on 2025-26 national incentives (see README) | 3 = strong purchase support, 1 = weak |
| `network_fit_score` (1-3) | Author's judgement: proximity to Fastned's existing network | 3 = borders a mature Fastned market |
| Fastned facts | [Q4 2025 results](https://www.fastnedcharging.com/hq/en/fastned-reports-381m-revenue-in-q4-as-charging-network-scale-up-accelerates--/), [Frontiers: new markets](https://www.fastnedcharging.com/en/stories/fastned-frontiers-new-markets), [electrive Q3 2026](https://www.electrive.com/2026/10/16/fastned-increases-charging-revenue-and-accelerates-network-expansion/) | 434 stations (Jun 2026), 9 countries, target 1,000 stations by 2030 |

Data years differ (sales 2025, chargers 2023, fleet 2024). The charger counts understate today's networks; the analysis uses them for relative comparison only.
